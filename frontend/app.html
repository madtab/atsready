FAQ

Common questions

What is an ATS and why does it matter?
An Applicant Tracking System is software used by most employers to automatically screen resumes before a recruiter ever sees them. If your resume uses fancy formatting, columns, tables, or graphics, the ATS often can't read it properly — and your application gets rejected regardless of your qualifications.
Why can't I just save my resume as a .docx myself?
Simply converting the file format isn't enough. ATS systems struggle with multi-column layouts, text boxes, headers/footers, and decorative elements. Our tool restructures your content into a proven single-column format with proper heading hierarchy that ATS parsers reliably understand.
How fast will I receive my converted resume?
Typically within 2-5 minutes of payment confirmation. The conversion is fully automated — no human in the loop means no waiting.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Your uploaded PDF is processed by our automated system and deleted immediately after conversion. No human views your resume, and we don't store any personal data beyond what's needed to deliver your result.
What if my PDF is image-based (scanned)?
Our tool works best with PDFs that contain selectable text. If your resume is a scanned image, the text extraction will be limited. In that case, we'll let you know and offer a full refund.
Can I get a refund?
If for any reason the conversion doesn't work properly — for example if your PDF is image-based — we'll refund you in full, no questions asked. Email us at hello@atsready.co.uk.
